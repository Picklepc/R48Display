# R48Display v0.3.7

**OTA from v0.3.0 or later is safe** — the partition table is unchanged.

## Pay timekeeping reworked for accuracy
- Pay now measures working time from a **working-session log** — the actual blades-on spans — instead of whole-day totals. Pay periods are exact to the second, so you can run **two separate pay periods on the same day**: one child mows your lawn in the morning, another mows a neighbor's in the afternoon, and each is paid for only their own mowing time.
- The current day's mowing now counts **live** toward pay and the activity grid, instead of only appearing after midnight.
- Pay is fully independent of the activity heatmap: working hours are recorded whenever **Enable Pay Records** is on, even with daily-activity tracking off.

## Correction tool for splitting and back-dating periods
- **Period Start** is now a date **and time**, and can be back-dated to fix a period you forgot to reset before someone mowed.
- New **"start at working-hours into a day"** option: begin a period after, say, the first 1.5 hours of a day's mowing — the firmware converts that to the exact moment from the session log.
- **Mark Paid** gains a **"Close at"** field: leave it blank to close now (all hours so far), or enter *N* working-hours of today to split the day — the next period picks up exactly where this one ends, so no mowing time is lost or double-counted.
- Payment history and the period start now show the date **and time**.

## Notes
- Precise pay tracking begins with this update. Earlier days have only the heatmap's whole-day totals, so the hours-based tools are exact for recent days and unavailable for older ones. Closing out any open pay period after updating gives a clean start.
- The working-session log stores one span per mowing session (not minute-by-minute samples) and is capped at roughly six months of history (a few kilobytes of storage).

R48Display Installer

Flash your Waveshare ESP32-S3 display straight from this page — no software to install.

  1. Plug the display into this computer with a USB-C data cable (not a charge-only cable).
  2. Click Install R48Display below.
  3. Pick the device's serial port in the pop-up and let it flash. It reboots when done.

Your browser can't flash over USB. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer.

 

Already set up? Once the device is on your Wi-Fi, you don't need this page again — it checks for and installs its own updates from its web dashboard.

Re-flashing here keeps your saved settings (Wi-Fi, BMS, hours). Your data lives in a separate storage area the installer doesn't touch.
